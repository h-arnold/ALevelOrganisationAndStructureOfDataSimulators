Summary & Evaluation

Key Concepts

  • Terminator Method: Requires a scan of every byte to find the marker. High I/O overhead.
  • Length Header Method: Reads the size, then skips data. Much lower I/O (fewer bytes processed), but still serial access.
  • Direct Access (Fixed): Instant Calculation. Zero search overhead.

🤔 Efficiency Challenge

Why don't we just use "Length Header" variable records for everything?

Click for Answer

Calculating the address of the 500th record is still impossible without reading the previous 499 length headers! You still have to 'walk' the chain, even if you are jumping. Fixed Length lets you teleport.
